Detailed Description

TXT exporter class, values separated by ';'.

Sparse format: name.txt (row;column;value) and name_Ap/_Ai/_Ax.txt (compressed arrays). Dense format: name.txt (one line per row). A linearized system is always exported in the sparse format, in files prefixed by linearization_t.
